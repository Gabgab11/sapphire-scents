Affordable luxury, made in Nigeria

Sapphire Scents was founded in 2015 to make affordable luxury perfumes for Africans all over the world.

“My destiny is to put Africa and Nigeria on the world map when it comes to perfumes.”

Sapphire Scents blends African indigenous ingredients with French and oriental oils: roses from Morocco, frankincense from Ethiopia, lavender from Europe. The result is perfume with the depth of oud and the polish of French perfumery, made to suit African climates and African skin.

The house was started in 2015 by Adewale Aladejana, known in perfumery as Wale Jana, with very little starting capital and a lot of conviction. Many of its fragrances carry Yoruba names and personal stories: Asiko means time, Kadara means destiny, Saki means royalty and favour, and Ruby is named after his first daughter.

Today Sapphire Scents is sold by more than 2,000 distributors across 30 Nigerian states and in countries around the world, and its fragrances are listed on Fragrantica.

Wale Jana is the founder and CEO, and the author of the book Scars.
