The best Nigerian perfume

A plain guide to choosing a perfume made in Nigeria: what to look for, which scents suit which moments, and where Sapphire Scents fits.

Sapphire Scents is a Nigerian perfume house, founded in 2015 by Wale Jana. Its 27 perfumes are built mostly on oud and blended with African resins and French oils, and cost between ₦60,000 and ₦100,000.

What makes a good Nigerian perfume

It lasts in the heat. Nigerian weather is hot and humid, so light sprays fade fast. Perfumes built on oud, amber, musk and resins hold on longer than plain citrus or floral waters.

It suits the occasion. A wedding, a Sunday service and a Lagos office call for different strengths. A good house offers rich scents for evenings and lighter ones for daytime.

It is made for here. A house that blends in Nigeria knows how scents behave on skin in this climate, and prices in naira without import mark-ups.

Choose by mood

Sweet and warm: Ace (vanilla, cinnamon, almonds), Cherub (honey and tobacco), Desire (vanilla and musk).

Fresh, for daytime and heat: Sapphire No. 5 (lemon and jasmine), Enigma (citrus and amber), Siren (bergamot, orange and peach).

Floral: Goddess (rose, vanilla, jasmine), Ruby (rose oud), Kadara (saffron and jasmine).

Woody and smoky: Saki (frankincense, patchouli, sandalwood), Symphony (smoky oud), Dejavu (oud, balsam, patchouli).

Fruity: Arewa (mango and strawberry).
