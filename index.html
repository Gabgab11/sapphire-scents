The House
“My destiny is to put Africa and Nigeria on the world map when it comes to perfumes.”

Sapphire Scents was founded in 2015 by Adewale Aladejana, known in perfumery as Wale Jana, its founder and creative director. Many of the house's names come from Yoruba: Asiko means time, Kadara means destiny.

The house blends African resins such as Ethiopian frankincense with French and oriental materials, and keeps reworking its formulas from client and distributor feedback.
